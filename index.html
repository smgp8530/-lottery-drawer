🎟️ Oregon Lottery Cash Drawer

Manual daily-report totals · Flexible employee shifts · Save & print closing reports

Important: Enter cumulative totals from the official Lottery current-day cash report. The report resets at 2:30 AM. If a shift crosses that reset, record a pre-reset snapshot and a post-reset snapshot; the app cannot recover missing totals. This app does not validate tickets or connect to Lottery terminals.

🔐 Manager Setup & Employee Management

First-time setup: create a manager PIN. Afterward, unlock management with that PIN to add employees, assign PINs, and review shortages. PINs are stored only in this browser; this is not secure multi-device authentication.
